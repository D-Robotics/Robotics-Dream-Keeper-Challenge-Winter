# Awards, Titles, and Recognition

This document defines the Winter stage progression, community advocacy recognition, and special awards. Organizer decisions, local eligibility rules, taxes, and shipping restrictions apply.

## Stage Progression

| Stage | Title | How to earn it | Unlocks | Winter reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Registration** | Official participant | Submit the form, join the Discord challenge channel, post an introduction, and activate RDK Studio. | Stage 1 access and program support | Entry into a draw for one of **three RDK stereo cameras**; official participant list |
| **Stage 1** | **RDK Explorer** | Complete all Stage 1 tasks, publish the required report, and add all links to the participant's Discord thread. | Stage 2 and the RDK Explorer Discord role | Official recognition and one participant T-shirt |
| **Stage 2** | **RDK Builder** | Complete all Stage 2 tasks, publish the required report, and update the Discord thread. | Stage 3 and the RDK Builder Discord role | One hour of 1-on-1 technical support; selected projects invited to Show & Tell |
| **Stage 3** | **RDK Creator** | Complete all Stage 3 tasks, publish the complete project package, and update the Discord thread. | RDK Creator Discord role | **USD 150**, e-certificate, physical Robotics Dream Keeper medal, and official exposure |

Physical rewards and cash awards are planned for distribution within one month after the program, subject to the final organizer announcement.

## RDK Advocator

Participants may apply for RDK Advocator recognition by publishing challenge-related content **more than eight times** outside D-Robotics-owned communities during the 45-day competition.

Each qualifying item must:

1. Clearly mention **D-Robotics**, **RDK**, or **RDK Challenge**.
2. Include at least one referral link to an official D-Robotics website, repository, or social channel.

Eligible formats include social posts, YouTube or TikTok videos, technical blogs, community presentations, and school, company, or team sharing sessions.

**Recognition and reward:** RDK Advocator Discord identity plus a merchandise set containing one mug, one pair of socks, one mousepad, and one sticker.

## TOP Voice Award

Three winners are selected from eligible RDK Advocators:

- **Writer:** strongest technical writing and insight suitable for official citation.
- **Producer:** strongest video production and visual storytelling, considering both judging and audience response.
- **Influencer:** highest verified total exposure across published campaign content.

Each winner receives:

- **USD 500**.
- TOP Voice certificate and Discord identity.
- Invitation to share in the global livestream.
- Priority consideration for official features and longer-term community collaboration.

## TOP Creator Award

Three winners are selected from participants who complete the full challenge. Judges consider:

1. Delivery completeness.
2. Technical difficulty.
3. Innovation.
4. Community contribution.
5. Overall ecosystem impact.

Each winner receives:

- **USD 1,000**.
- TOP Creator certificate / trophy and Discord identity.
- Invitation to the global livestream.
- Official feature and collaboration opportunities.

The global TOP Creator package may also include a Shenzhen innovation trip, DGP incubation consideration, a global developer interview, and official collaboration opportunities. Final travel and eligibility terms will be announced separately.

## Eligibility and Fair Play

- One primary showcase file per participant per season unless a team is registered in advance.
- Work must be original. Forks and tutorials must contain substantive new engineering or integration.
- Dangerous demos without documented safeguards are ineligible.
- All code, models, datasets, and media must comply with their licenses.
- Submission links must remain accessible through the judging period.
- The judging panel has final responsibility for edge cases and award interpretation.
