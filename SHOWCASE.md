## Previous Season: 2026 Summer

While the Winter wall is being built, explore the official [2026 Summer project wall](https://github.com/D-Robotics/Robotics-Dream-Keeper-Challenge/blob/develop/SHOWCASE.md). It includes 11 RDK X5 projects across navigation, perception, speech, manipulation, patrol, education, cleaning, and edge AI.

The Summer TOP Creator projects are useful references for technical depth and presentation quality:

- [Tri-Cam NavBot](https://github.com/D-Robotics/Robotics-Dream-Keeper-Challenge/blob/develop/projects/MuhirwaRichard-Project-TriCamNavBot.md) — encoderless indoor navigation with scan matching, gyro fusion, multi-camera perception, and concurrent BPU workloads
- [BETL-001](https://github.com/D-Robotics/Robotics-Dream-Keeper-Challenge/blob/develop/projects/IsaacRF-Project-BETL-001.md) — a bio-inspired mobile robot combining BPU detection, dead reckoning, lidar reconstruction, and layered safety
- [Korosuke](https://github.com/D-Robotics/Robotics-Dream-Keeper-Challenge/blob/develop/projects/KazukiMurata-Project-Korosuke.md) — an on-device animatronic robot combining vision, speech, local language models, audio, and expressive motion
